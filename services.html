Our Services

Flytech Aerospace provides aircraft line maintenance operations for operators that need responsive, compliant, and reliable support.

01

AOG Support

When an aircraft is grounded, Flytech coordinates fast, safety-focused support to assess the issue, organize the required action, and help return the aircraft to service with proper documentation.

  • 24/7/365 operational response coordination
  • Aircraft-on-ground troubleshooting support
  • Defect assessment and maintenance planning
  • Parts, tooling, and technical coordination
  • Return-to-service documentation support
02

Line Maintenance

Our line maintenance support is built for day-to-day aircraft operation, helping operators maintain dispatch reliability through routine checks, troubleshooting, and turnaround assistance.

  • Transit, turnaround, and overnight support
  • Pre-flight and post-flight inspections
  • Routine line checks and corrective actions
  • Component replacement and minor repairs
  • Maintenance records and operator coordination
03

Engine Installations

Flytech supports engine installation work with structured planning, technical coordination, inspection awareness, and a compliance-first approach from preparation through operational readiness.

  • Engine removal and installation support
  • Installation planning and task coordination
  • Engine interface and accessory support
  • Inspection checkpoints during installation
  • Post-installation readiness coordination
